4. How the demo makes its estimate

Show model details and limitations

Your notebook selects Logistic Regression. Its features are age, distance_km, fee_inr, lead_days, prior_no_shows, reminder_sent, booking_channel and visit_type. The notebook reports a test ROC-AUC of about 0.789 and uses a 0.35 threshold after comparing thresholds.

This standalone HTML does not load your trained Python model. It uses the coefficient directions and relative weights shown in the notebook, with illustrative feature scaling and a baseline anchored near the project's 22% no-show rate. Because the fitted scaler values and model intercept were not saved in the notebook output, its percentages are illustrative—not exact model predictions and not suitable for real patient decisions.

For exact predictions, export the fitted preprocessing pipeline and model from Python (for example, with joblib) and connect the HTML to a prediction API. This demo runs locally in your browser and does not send patient data anywhere.
